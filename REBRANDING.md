# Adapt SmiCalc for your company

**Yes — you can use and adapt SmiCalc under your own brand, including commercially.**
You can keep your changes private. Sharing improvements back is welcome but optional.

## What can I change?

- Your company name, logo, colours and contact information.
- Links to your products, catalogue and website.
- Interface text and the features you offer.
- Cutting data and calculations, with appropriate engineering review and testing.

You may offer the customized app to customers or distributors. A visible SmiCut
name, logo or link is not required in your version.

## How do I start?

1. [Download your language](README.md#choose-your-language) and keep an unchanged backup.
2. Decide what to change: for example, your logo, brand colours and product links.
3. Give the HTML file and your requirements to your web developer, or edit it yourself
   if you know HTML, CSS and JavaScript. There is no built-in branding settings panel.
4. Test the modified app on a computer and phone. Verify any changed cutting data
   and calculations before use.
5. Share the file or [publish it on your website](docs/getting-started.md#use-smicalc-on-your-website).

For changes across several languages, developers should use the shared source and
translation workflow in [the development guide](docs/development.md).

## What must I keep?

Keep SmiCut AB's copyright notice and the complete [MIT license](LICENSE) with
copies you distribute. The downloaded HTML already contains these in a comment
at the beginning of the file. Leave that comment in place; it is not displayed in
the app. Include `LICENSE` when distributing a multi-file package.

Present modifications as your own version. Do not imply that SmiCut has approved
your changes. Permission to adapt the software does not grant exclusive rights
to SmiCut's name or branding.

## Can I use the AI Material Selector too?

The calculators can be used without the AI service. Access to SmiCut's hosted AI
Material Selector is separate from the software license. For your own website,
[request approval from SmiCut](MATERIAL_SELECTOR.md). Once approved, no API key
needs to be added. The normal download does not enable AI access when opened
as a file on your computer.
